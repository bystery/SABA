The Crime: Joe Farmer was attacked from behind in a muddy corn maze, and a diamond engagement ring was stolen from his hand.

Physical Evidence (Footprints): Joe observed two sets of tracks in the mud: his own deep footprints and a second set belonging to the attacker.

Track Analysis: The attacker's footprints were shallower and closer together than Joe’s, indicating the culprit was lighter and shorter than Joe.

Suspect 1 (Nick): The oldest brother. He is four inches taller and nearly fifty pounds heavier than Joe, which contradicts the shallow, short-strided footprints found at the scene.

Suspect 2 (Austin): The second oldest brother. He is at least two inches taller than Joe and shares Nick's large build, also making him too heavy and tall to be the source of the tracks.

Suspect 3 (Billy): The youngest brother. He is the only one lighter and shorter than Joe, matching the physical profile suggested by the footprints.

The Alibi Contradiction: Billy claimed he got lost in the corn maze while looking for Joe. However, Joe pointed out that the brothers grew up on the farm and knew the maze too well to get lost.

The Muddy Trail: Joe realized it was easy for Billy to follow the deep, fresh muddy prints Joe left behind, rendering his claim of being "lost" a lie.

Motive: All three brothers had feelings for Maria Irene and were jealous that she had chosen Joe.

The Reveal: Based on the size and depth of the footprints and the impossibility of an experienced farm boy getting lost in a familiar maze, Joe identified Billy as the thief.
