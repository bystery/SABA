Physical Evidence (The Santa Suit): The Secret Santa wore a full costume to conceal his identity in case he was spotted in the sanctuary.

The Key Holders: Beyond the staff, only five male congregation members held keys to the main building: Al Busby, Chuck Daughtry, Bobby Key, Jeff Reynolds, and Jim Dockery.

Movement and Speed: The Pastor observed the Secret Santa moving "quickly and silently" and with "a great deal of grace."

Suspect 1 (Al Busby): Ruled out because he is 77 years old and suffers from arthritic knees, requiring a cane to walk. He could not have moved with the speed observed.

Suspect 2 (Chuck Daughtry): Ruled out because the Secret Santa was seen carrying a "huge sack" using both hands. Chuck had recently suffered a badly broken arm during a basketball game.

Suspect 3 (Bobby Key): Ruled out due to his extreme clumsiness (having recently broken a leg and a lamp). The Secret Santa’s movements were described as graceful and efficient.

Suspect 4 (Jeff Reynolds): Ruled out because a stroke the previous year left him with a limp and physical weakness on his right side, making the quick, agile movements of "Santa" impossible for him.

The "Left-Handed" Clue: On the third night, Pastor Dan observed the Secret Santa reaching into the gift sack specifically with his left hand.

Personal History: Pastor Dan recalled a lunch with Jim Dockery where Jim mentioned he was the first left-handed pitcher on his minor league baseball team to throw a no-hitter.

The Reveal: Jim Dockery’s physical fitness as a former athlete, his access to the building, and his left-handedness confirmed he was the Secret Santa.

Outcome: Jim admitted to the acts, explaining he wanted to perform his "good works in secret" to keep the focus on the spiritual meaning of Christmas.
