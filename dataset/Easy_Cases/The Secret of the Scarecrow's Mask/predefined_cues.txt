Setting: A small Scottish castle miles from the nearest town, featuring a large armor exhibit and guest rooms on the top floor.

The Prime Exhibit: The legendary "Scarecrow Mask," which belonged to an outlaw who once escaped capture by posing as a scarecrow in plain sight.

The Crime: The mask was stolen from its pole at the center of the armor exhibit shortly before the exhibit was set to open.

Suspect 1 (Mrs. Winfrey): Suggested as a suspect by Charles Kincaid, but ruled out because she was completely unfamiliar with the legend of where the outlaw had hidden.

Suspect 2 (Mr. Winfrey): Ruled out after a thorough search of his room and his many bags revealed no sign of the mask.

Suspect 3 (Charles Kincaid): A descendant of the disgraced sheriff who failed to catch the outlaw. Although he had a motive of "family revenge," a search of his room, his fine suits, and his pockets turned up nothing.

Suspect 4 (Chester): Bertram’s son and the castle's cook, who lived in the separate cook's quarters located outside, behind the castle.

External Entry: The detective deduced the thief came from outside because of a trail of wet footprints leading from the scarecrow's pole. None of the guests had wet hair or signs of being out in the storm.

Physical Evidence 1 (Wet Shoes): The thief’s shoes were wet from the walk between the outdoor quarters and the castle, leaving a clear trail of tracks.

Physical Evidence 2 (Potato Peel): A "wet and slimy" white scrap found on the west staircase was identified as a potato peel.

The "Potato" Clue: The detective and Bertram realized the potato peel was a definitive link to the cook (Chester), who had been preparing and serving potatoes for dinner.

Unsearched Area: Chester’s room in the outdoor cook's quarters was the only room that was not searched by the detective and Bertram.

The Motive: Chester wanted to sabotage the castle's tourist trade because he was unhappy living in the "cold and unpleasant" cook's quarters and wanted to discourage visitors.

The Reveal: Chester was identified as the thief because of the trail of footprints from the pole and the tell-tale potato peel he accidentally dropped while escaping the scene.
