“While each of us touched your purse, that does not mean that we each had a chance to take your money. Even if Tammy has seven singles, unless Tammy can travel in time, I don’t see how she could have taken your change before you even got it.”
“Next is the Munchkin. Since he only has a ten-dollar bill, he obviously does not have your four singles. Likewise, I only have a five and two-ones, so I did not take it. We’ve settled that none of us took your money, and that you didn’t lose it, right? Then that leaves just one other person who touched your money tonight.”
Kasey still looked blank, so Juliet continued.
“Hand me your money please – and don’t worry, I’ll give it right back to you.”
Hesitantly, Kasey gave her the four bills.
Juliet demonstrated carefully, first folding the stack of bills in half, then counting down the ends one by one. “One – two – three – four – five – six – seven – eight. Here’s your eight dollars, ma’am.”
Kasey gasped as she took the money back. “Cody!” she shouted. “He distracted me with his charm while he stole my money!”
Tammy and Juliet both rolled their eyes, but nodded in agreement. The four hurried back to Happy Burger, and found Cody’s manager. He fired the unfortunate thief, gave Kasey her four dollars back, and offered them all complimentary drinks as an apology.
They made it back to the theater in record time, missing only most of the previews, and when the flesh-crazed zombies turned out to be uproariously funny, they all wound up laughing Coke out their noses. Except for Billy that is – he fell asleep.
