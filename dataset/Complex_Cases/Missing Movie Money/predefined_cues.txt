Transaction Details: Kasey paid for a kids' meal and her order with a twenty-dollar bill.


Calculated Change: The cashier, Cody, stated the change should be eight dollars and forty-two cents ($8.42).


Cashier's Maneuver: Cody held up a folded stack of bills and counted slowly to eight as he flipped through the ends of the bills.


Kasey’s Observation: After counting the bills, Cody handed them to Kasey with a wink.


The Discovery: At the theater, Kasey discovered she only had four singles instead of the expected eight.


Kasey’s Suspicion: Kasey initially accused her friends and brother, Billy, because they had each held her purse.


Suspect 1 (Billy): Billy turned out his pockets to reveal only a ten-dollar bill given to him by his mother.


Suspect 2 (Juliet): Juliet showed she had exactly seven dollars (a five and two ones) for her ticket.


Suspect 3 (Tammy): Tammy produced seven single dollar bills for her ticket.


The Mechanical Trick: Juliet demonstrated that if a stack of four bills is folded in half, counting the loose ends one by one makes it appear as though there are eight bills.


The Distraction: Cody used his charm and flirting to distract Kasey while she slipped the money into her purse without verifying the count.

The True Thief: Cody intentionally shortchanged Kasey by four dollars using the folding trick.


Outcome: Cody’s manager fired him and returned the stolen four dollars to Kasey.
