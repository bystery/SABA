The Theft: A legendary and valuable saxophone (estimated between $20,000 and $30,000) was stolen from Dillon’s locked second-floor apartment.

The Legend: The saxophone was one of four historic instruments originally owned by jazz legends and managed by the notorious 1960s producer, Shorty Jenkins.

Room Security: Dillon’s only door was locked, and his window was closed. Though the window opened to a fire escape, the ladder to the ground was broken, and the flower bed below was undisturbed, ruling out entry via the fire escape.

Suspect 1 (Lenny the Delivery Boy): Lenny has a key to the apartment but works all day under his father’s "no breaks" policy. He also expressed a lack of interest in saxophones, viewing them as worthless in the modern neighborhood.

Suspect 2 (Eric the Student): Eric knew the instrument’s true value through his research, but he only had Dillon’s phone number (obtained from Red) and did not know Dillon’s home address.

Suspect 3 (Red the Music Store Owner): Red has extensive knowledge of the quartet but gave music lessons from noon to five, providing an alibi for the time of the theft. Furthermore, he had no way to enter the locked apartment.

The Master Key: The only other person with a key to the apartment was the mysterious building manager, whom the tenants "never see around."

Physical Description: Red described a customer in his store during Eric’s discussion: a very old man, about five feet tall, bald, with a bristly moustache, who "owns a lot of buildings."

The Reveal: The building manager fits the physical description perfectly (bald, short, bristly moustache). He had a key, heard Dillon practicing daily, and recognized the songs.

The Identity of the Thief: The building manager is actually the former producer, Shorty Jenkins. He used his master key to reclaim the legendary instrument he had provided decades ago.

Motive: As the original producer and a building owner, Jenkins knew the historic value of the saxophones and used his access to steal it back once he realized Dillon still had it.
