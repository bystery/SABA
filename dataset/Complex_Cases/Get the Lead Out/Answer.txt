Dan Skinner took Tro Nguyen’s mechanical pencil. He was the first one back into the audit room after lunch and simply picked the pencil up off Tro’s desk at the head of the row while his colleagues filed in behind him, unable to see what he was doing in the narrow room.
Since the pencil was clearly on Tro’s desk when the auditors left for lunch and locked the door to their room, the only other person who could’ve taken it was the company controller, Benjamin Trodger, because he had another key to the room. But he was tied up with Mr. Seldon, the company president, the entire time that the auditors were gone – as they witnessed on their way out of and back into the building, and as Mary Thomason, Mr. Seldon’s secretary, verbally confirmed.
