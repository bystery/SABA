Audit Room Layout: The audit team used a small, narrow mail/supply room where five desks were arranged in a single-file row.


Tro’s Desk Position: Tro Nguyen’s desk was the one located closest to the room’s only door.


The Gold Pencil: Tro possessed an expensive gold-trimmed mechanical pencil that was left lying on his desk when the team departed for lunch.


Room Security: Steve Clairborne locked the door with a key before lunch, ensuring the room was secure.


Access Keys: Only two keys to the room existed: one held by Steve and one held by the company controller, Benjamin Trodger.


Benjamin Trodger’s Alibi: Trodger was in a lunch conference with the company president, Mr. Seldon, the entire time the auditors were away.


Alibi Confirmation: The auditors saw Trodger in Seldon's office both when leaving and returning; additionally, Seldon's secretary confirmed they had been together non-stop for over an hour.


Dan Skinner’s Motive: Before lunch, the lead in Dan’s own mechanical pencil snapped and jammed, rendering it useless.


Dan’s Opportunity: Upon returning from lunch, Steve handed the key to Dan, making him the first person to enter the cramped, narrow room.


Concealment: Because the room was narrow and the desks were in a single-file line, the colleagues filing in behind Dan could not see him pick up the pencil from the first desk.


The Thief Identified: Dan Skinner took the pencil because he was the only person with the opportunity and a broken pencil that needed replacing.
