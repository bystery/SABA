Setting: The Easter egg hunt took place in a large field behind the church on a sunny, warm, but windy Saturday morning. 


The Prize: A special golden egg was hidden in the field, with an extra bag of candy as a reward for the finder. 


Secret Location: Only Karen (the overseer) and Karla (the volunteer) knew the golden egg was hidden behind the largest oak tree in the far left corner of the field. 


Suspect 1 (Cole Bryant): Throughout the hunt, Cole stayed focused on the evergreen shrubbery in the middle of the field. 


Suspect 2 (Rachel Smithson): Rachel spent her time hunting in the rock pile located to the right of the evergreen shrubbery. 


Suspect 3 (Anna Laughlin): Near the end of the hunt, Anna became upset about her low egg count and stayed close to Karla until the closing whistle blew. 


Suspect 4 (Justin Bates): Justin remained in the sand pit in the far right corner of the field for the entire duration of the hunt. 


Critical Witness Observation: While Karen was inside the church, Karla had to warn Lizzie to be careful of dead branches falling from the big oak tree. 


Consistency Check: When Karen returned, Karla confirmed that none of the children had moved from one side of the field to the other while Karen was away. 


Physical Proximity: Lizzie was the only child observed near the big oak tree, which was the actual hiding spot of the golden egg. 


The Deduction: Since all other children were confirmed to be in areas far away from the oak tree (shrubbery, rock pile, sand pit, or near Karla) for the duration of the hunt, only Lizzie had the opportunity to find the egg. 


Outcome: Lizzie found the golden egg but selflessly gave it to her friend Anna, who had struggled during the hunt.
