Environmental Context: The story takes place on a hot July day during Paula's birthday celebration at her office.


The Crime: Paula's large chocolate birthday cupcake was stolen and eaten between the morning celebration and lunchtime.


The Cupcake's Location: Paula placed the cupcake in a paper sack on the "top shelf" of the office kitchen, pushing it as far back as she could reach while standing on her toes.


Evidence Found: Geraldine led Paula to Luis’s cubicle, where the empty brown paper bag was found in his wastebasket.


Suspect 1 (Mr. Bento): The boss is on a "strict diet" and watching his weight; he explicitly stated he did not take it.


Suspect 2 (Julianna): Although she was hostile toward Paula, she is described as being "under four feet tall". Paula concluded Julianna was too short to reach the top shelf where the cupcake was hidden.


Suspect 3 (Luis): Luis arrived back at the office "flushed and saturated in sweat" from running errands outdoors in the hot July weather. He claimed he had been gone all morning and questioned why he would steal a gift he bought himself.


Suspect 4 (Geraldine): When first seen in the morning, Geraldine smiled at Paula, revealing "pearly white teeth".


The Alibi Contradiction: When accused later that morning, Geraldine claimed she could not have eaten the cupcake because she "forgot [her] teeth" and showed Paula her "toothless gums".


The Logical Flaw: Paula remembered Geraldine having her teeth in during the morning surprise party. This proves Geraldine lied about being unable to chew the cupcake.


The Frame-up: Geraldine was the one who "knew" the bag was in Luis's wastebasket and led everyone there, suggesting she planted the evidence to frame him.


The Thief Identified: Geraldine stole and ate the cupcake, then removed her dentures to create a false alibi.
