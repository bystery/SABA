The office access system is a high-security turnstile that allows only one person to enter per card swipe and is directly linked to the company's computer system.

The IT manager, Jeffery Sharp, has limited access to the security system but is authorized to synchronize it for events like leap years or date changes.

Jeffery Sharp claimed he was up all night packing for a vacation to Aruba and was not at the office after the party.

Jeffery provided a printout showing only two people entered the office that morning: Donna Blake (6:10 a.m.) and George Wilson (6:25 a.m.).

Donna Blake noted a "computer glitch" where her reports were automatically being dated with tomorrow's date instead of today's.

The janitor, Pete Thompson, claimed he worked from 1:00 a.m. to 4:00 a.m. to clean the office, yet his name did not appear on the morning access report.

The head of security, Mr. Connelly, stated that the local IT manager cannot shut down the system without blocking all entry and exit.

A second access report for the previous two days showed Pete entering at 1:07 a.m. and Jeffery entering at 4:15 a.m..

This second report was erroneously dated "today," while the report showing Wilson and Blake was dated "tomorrow".

The detective realized that the thief manipulated the computer system's date to hide their entry.

By moving the system date forward one day, Jeffery made his 4:15 a.m. entry (the night of the theft) appear as if it happened on a future date, effectively "hiding" it from a standard check of the current day's logs.

The final proof was that the date on Jeffery’s airline tickets did not match the date on the access report he provided for the day of the robbery.
