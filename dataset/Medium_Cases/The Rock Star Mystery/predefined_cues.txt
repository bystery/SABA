The Crime: A gold record was stolen from the den of Gorg Brewer’s mansion. The theft allegedly occurred between midnight and 1 a.m.

The Setting: A rainy night in Hollywood. The mansion is surrounded by a muddy path that stains anyone’s footwear a "dirty brown."

Physical Evidence 1 (The Floor): Despite the heavy rain and the "very muddy path" leading to the door, the hallway floor was "spotless."

Physical Evidence 2 (The Diamond Guitar): A diamond-studded guitar sat in the hallway in plain sight. It was not stolen, even though it was arguably more valuable and easier to grab than the gold record in the den.

Suspect 1 (Stu the Delivery Person):

Opportunity: He delivered Chinese food at midnight.

Alibi/Exclusion: When Stu arrived with Lenny's egg rolls later that night, his hair was greasy from the rain and he had "tramped down the same muddy path." If he had entered the house at midnight to steal the record, he would have left a trail of mud on the "spotless" floor.

Suspect 2 (The Neighborhood Burglar):

Profile: He steals anything expensive.

Exclusion: An experienced burglar would not have walked past the diamond-studded guitar in the hallway just to steal a gold record hidden in the den.

Suspect 3 (Gorg Brewer):

Motive: Insurance fraud.

Exclusion: Gorg didn't even know the record was insured; his wife, Tina, was the one managing the estate and the insurance papers.

Suspect 4 (Tina):

Motive: She was the manager and knew the record was insured.

The Timing Flaw: Tina claimed she found the record missing after a movie ended (the movie was on the "late show"). However, she called the police at 1 a.m., yet told the officers the movie was over. If the movie was the "late show," it would typically end much later.

The Clean Floor: As the person living in the house, she could move around without tracking in mud from the outside path.

The Reveal: Tina stole the record to collect insurance money and tried to frame Stu by leaving the door "unlocked" and mentioning his name. Her lie about the timing and the lack of mud on the floor proved she was the inside thief.
