The Setting: Mary visits a magical meadow on Easter Sunday where she meets a group of Easter Bunnies practicing their hiding skills.

The Gift: After leaving the meadow and attending church, Mary discovers a surprise blue-and-yellow Easter egg in her pocket.

The Suspects: Abbot (the leader), Randy and Andy (twin brothers), and Speedy.

Suspect 1 (Abbot): He is the "boss" who supervises. When Mary asked for a souvenir, he specifically refused, stating, "All these eggs are spoken for... Not one more." He gave her a lily instead.

Suspect 2 & 3 (Randy and Andy):

They are identical twins with silver legs and grey fur.

Randy explicitly stated he hides "only dark grey eggs."

Abbot confirmed the twins are identical in every way: "Everything they do alike — walking, talking, hiding eggs." Since the gift was blue and yellow, it does not match their specific style or preference.

Suspect 4 (Speedy):

Physical Traits: Cloud-white fur and red eyes. He is described as the fastest rabbit.

The "Breeze" Clue: When Speedy moves, Mary feels "breezes brushed across her face."

The Delivery: While Mary was walking back toward the park, she noted that she "even felt some breezes." This indicates Speedy ran past her at high speed.

The Logic: Mary had empty pockets (except for her keys) when she left the meadow. The "breezes" she felt while walking back were caused by Speedy's rapid movement as he used his speed to surreptitiously slip the egg into her pocket without her seeing him.

The Reveal: Speedy used his incredible speed to deliver the surprise gift, living up to his reputation for being "surprising."
