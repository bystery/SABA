The Setting: Sherlock Holmes and Dr. Watson are attending a violin concert at a London theater, using tickets sent by an anonymous admirer.

The Target: Professor Moriarty hired "Fastidious Fred" Fielder, a master of disguise from Liverpool, to kill Holmes.

Security & Location: Holmes and Watson are seated in a special box directly adjacent to the Royal Box, where the Queen is scheduled to arrive during intermission.

Suspect 1 (The Hansom Cab Driver): Ruled out because he was only hired to deliver them to the theater. He made no plans to pick them up afterward, meaning he had no opportunity to follow through with a crime at the scene.

Suspect 2 (The Ticket Seller): Ruled out because theater policy forbids her from leaving her booth during the performance. She could not have entered the private box to commit the murder.

Suspect 3 (The Policeman): Ruled out as a trusted officer known to Inspector Lestrade and recognized by Holmes. He was stationed outside the Royal Box for security.

Suspect 4 (The Theater Usher):

Disguise: He fits the description of "Fastidious Fred," a man capable of dressing any part to blend into his surroundings.

Opportunity: He was the only person with authorized access to the private box during the performance.

The Method: During intermission, he served three drinks "on the house." Holmes deduced these drinks were poisoned.

The "Three Seats" Clue: Holmes learned from the ticket seller that three seats were purchased for their box. Since only Holmes and Watson were intended to be there, the third seat was reserved for the killer (the Usher) to monitor or finish the deed.

The Mastermind's Plot: Moriarty intended for the bodies to be discovered just as the Queen arrived, creating a massive public stir and a "triumph" for his criminal career.

The Reveal: Holmes recognized the "Fastidious" nature of the setup and the suspicious "complimentary" drinks, leading to the Usher's arrest before the poison could be consumed.

Outcome: Fastidious Fred was arrested, and Moriarty, watching from the audience, realized his plan had failed.
