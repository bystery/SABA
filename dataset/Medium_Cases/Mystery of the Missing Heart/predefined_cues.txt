The Crime: A large, gold-foil-covered cardboard heart (the central prop of the school play) went missing during the post-performance cleanup.

The Suspects: Eric (the lead actor), Jimmy and Jenny (twins), and Wendy LaRue.

Suspect 1 (The Twins - Jimmy & Jenny): Ruled out because of their lack of motive. Jimmy called the heart "ugly" and Jenny called it "gaudy." They had no desire to keep it as a treasure.

Suspect 2 (Wendy LaRue): Ruled out because of a lack of opportunity. She left the cleanup early to go to dinner with her parents 20 minutes after the play ended, while the heart was still seen by the others later during the cleanup.

Suspect 3 (Eric):

Motive: Eric expressed deep sadness about the play ending and a desire to "spend a little more time" on the stage, showing an emotional attachment to the experience.

The Physical Clue (Allergy): Eric is highly allergic to straw and breaks out in an itchy rash upon contact.

Suspicious Behavior: On an unusually warm spring day when everyone else wore shorts and T-shirts, Eric wore a long-sleeved shirt to hide his arms and was seen constantly scratching them.

The "Straw" Connection: The only items that were moved out of the building to the dumpsters at the end of the night were two man-sized, straw-stuffed scarecrows.

The Deduction: The heart was too large to sneak out past the teacher. Eric hid the heart inside one of the straw-stuffed scarecrows before they were hauled to the dumpsters. He later returned to the dumpster to retrieve it, coming into contact with the straw and triggering his allergic rash, which he tried to hide with long sleeves.

The Reveal: The detective confronted Eric about his long sleeves and his scratching. Eric confessed, admitting he wanted the heart as a keepsake.

Outcome: Eric agreed to return the "heart" to Mrs. Tillman before going home.
