The Crime: The front axle nuts on Matthew’s brand-new bicycle were deliberately loosened, causing the wheel to fly off and Matthew to crash while riding on rough trails.

The Suspects: Mark (little brother), String (the practical joker), Cory (the group leader), and David (the friend with the beat-up bike).

Suspect 1 (Mark): Matthew initially blamed him because they caught him riding the bike earlier. However, as String pointed out, Mark wouldn't have been "dumb" enough to ride a bike with a loosened front wheel if he were the one who had sabotaged it.

Suspect 2 (String): Known as a "big-time practical joker." However, he ruled himself out by noting that he lagged behind during the ride and missed the crash. He argued that the "point of a practical joke is to see it happen." Additionally, he claimed he wouldn't perform a stunt that could actually cause physical injury.

Suspect 3 (Cory): Matthew suspected him because he chose the bumpy trails and "resented" the new bike. However, Cory was already at the city park waiting for the others; he was never at Matthew’s house where the bike was kept.

Suspect 4 (David):

Motive: He was openly resentful of Matthew’s "precious" new bike and the fact that Matthew wouldn't let anyone touch or ride it.

Opportunity: While Matthew and String were playing video games in the basement, David was alone in the garage.

The Means: David explicitly told the boys he "used some tools in the garage" to fix his own flat tire. These same tools provided the "weapon" needed to loosen the axle nuts on Matthew's bike.

The Reveal: String deduced that David was the only person with the motive, the tools, and the private opportunity to sabotage the bike.

Outcome: David's "crimson" face confirmed his guilt. He admitted he wanted to "teach Matthew a lesson" for being a "knob" about the bike. Matthew conceded and agreed to let his friends ride the bike once it was fixed.
