The Setting: Five families are celebrating New Year’s Eve at a beach house in the Outer Banks.

The Incident: At the stroke of midnight, Aly (the only teenage girl) was spotted returning from behind a sand dune. She later confessed to her father, Tom, that she had shared a New Year’s kiss with one of the four teenage boys.

The Suspects: Danny, Reese, Jeremy, and RJ.

The Tradition/Rules:

"Winner stays on" for the pool and ping-pong tables.

World-famous chocolate chip cookies are served at exactly midnight; the children "pounce" on them immediately.

Suspect 1 (Danny) & Suspect 2 (Reese):

Danny was playing ping-pong and Reese was playing pool. Both had vowed to play from dinner until sunrise without losing.

The younger children were watching and waiting to "unseat the champs."

If either had left the table at midnight, they would have forfeited their game/streak, which would have caused a "loud celebration" among the younger kids. Since no such celebration or loss was noted, they must have been at their respective tables at midnight.

Suspect 3 (Jeremy):

When Tom went downstairs shortly after midnight, Jeremy greeted him with his "face covered in chocolate."

Since the cookies were strictly off-limits until the clock struck twelve and were finished within "a minute or two," Jeremy must have been in the playroom at midnight to get his share of the cookies.

The Culprit (RJ):

By process of elimination, RJ is the only teenage boy whose presence in the playroom at midnight was not accounted for by a continuous game or the cookie pounce.

The Reveal: Diane (the mother) realized the answer was obvious based on the chocolate on Jeremy's face and the ongoing games of Danny and Reese.

Outcome: RJ was the one behind the sand dune with Aly.
