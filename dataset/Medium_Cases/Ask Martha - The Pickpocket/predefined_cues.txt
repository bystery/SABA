Crime Wave: A series of pickpocket incidents occurred in Centerville involving Sam Cartwright, Charley Eastern, and Mayor Greenleaf.

The Modus Operandi: In all cases, only cash was taken, credit cards were left untouched, and the wallets were discarded nearby.

The Suspects: Four high school drama students—Sarah Browne, Morris Emerson, Johnny Anderson, and Tom Blankenship—were present during each incident.

The Motive: The students were practicing for their upcoming production of Oliver Twist. They intended to "test" their skills as pickpockets and return the money on opening night as a publicity stunt.

Incident 1 (Safeway - Charley Eastern):

Suspect Activity: Sarah was at the movies (Alibi). Johnny and Morris were inside the store.

Key Observation: Tom Blankenship was seen leaving the Safeway exit just before the victim.

Incident 2 (Home Depot - Sam Cartwright):

Suspect Activity: Johnny was involved in a loud argument several aisles away.

Suspect Activity: Morris went over to the argument to calm things down, attracting significant attention to himself.

Suspect Activity: Sarah and Tom were the next customers in the checkout line behind the victim.

Incident 3 (Jewelry Shop - Mayor Greenleaf):

Suspect Activity: Sarah, Morris, and Johnny were already inside the shop.

Key Observation: Tom was outside parking the car and followed the Mayor into the store.

Timing: The Mayor's wallet was taken from his inside jacket pocket and found on the sidewalk immediately after he left.

Exclusion of Sarah: Sarah was at the movies during the Safeway theft, proving she was not the thief in that instance.

Exclusion of Johnny and Morris: At the Home Depot, Johnny was distracted by an argument and Morris was drawing attention to himself as a mediator; neither was in a position to discreetly lift a wallet.

The Culprit (Tom Blankenship):

Tom was the only one consistently in the right physical position during all three thefts (leaving the Safeway exit, standing behind Cartwright at Home Depot, and following the Mayor into the Jewelry Shop).

Fittingly, Tom plays the "Artful Dodger" in their play—the character famous for being a master pickpocket.

The Confession: The students mailed a signed confession and returned the money before the detective's column was even published, showing genuine contrition.
