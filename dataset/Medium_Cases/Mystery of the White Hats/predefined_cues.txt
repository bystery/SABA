The Setting: A memorial service for Ernie, a former Navy sailor, held on Memorial Day in Montana in 1959.

The Running Joke: During WWII, 18-year-old Ernie jokingly claimed to be married to the famous movie star Betty Grable to deflect teasing from his shipmates. The prank became legendary on their Navy cruiser, even acknowledged by their captain.

The "Miracle": At the funeral, a woman resembling Betty Grable appeared in a black veil and dress. She signed the guest book and fled in a limousine before anyone could speak to her.

Suspect 1 (Captain Stark): Ruled out because he only received two hours' notice about the service, which was not enough time to arrange such an elaborate celebrity appearance or prank.

Suspect 2 (Doug): Ruled out because he was genuinely shocked and spent the afternoon aggressively questioning Ernie’s family. If he had been the mastermind, he wouldn't have been so embarrassed or taken the "mystery" so seriously.

Suspect 3 (Ernie): Ruled out because he died unexpectedly in his sleep and had no way to pre-plan a prank for his own memorial service.

Suspect 4 (Chet):

Background: He was from California and claimed to have known and socialized with movie stars.

The Guest Book: Chet was the one who slipped out during the service to "help the minister's wife set up the guest book."

The Evidence: The guest book was not set out until after the service had already started. The mysterious woman left immediately as the service ended.

The Logical Flaw: The real Betty Grable was married to Harry James at the time (1943–1965) and would be unlikely to sign her stage name "Betty Grable" in a formal funeral guest book.

The Reveal: Chet, being the only one with access to the guest book during the service and the one who claimed movie star connections, forged the signature. He likely hired or convinced a woman (possibly an actress friend from his time in Hollywood) to make a brief, silent appearance to honor Ernie's lifelong joke.

Conclusion: The "miracle" was a final tribute orchestrated by Chet to ensure Ernie's famous story had a legendary ending.
