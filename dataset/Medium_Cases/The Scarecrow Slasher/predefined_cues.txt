The Crime: Four consecutive nights of "Scarecrow Slashings" on the Forbes estate. The scarecrows were not just torn, but "viciously sabotaged" with a knife, including the wooden support poles being cut clean through.

The Setting: An old estate rumored to have hidden treasure belonging to Mr. Forbes’ explorer grandfather.

Timing of the Final Attack: The maid (Annie) witnessed a figure attack the scarecrow at 11:20 PM. This matches the exact time Mr. Forbes was on the phone reporting the crime to the police.

Suspect 1 (Mr. Forbes): Ruled out because he was on the phone with the police at the exact moment the "slasher" was spotted by the maid.

Suspect 2 (The Cook - Mrs. Avery): Ruled out because the maid described the attacker as "very agile" and moving "like a cat." Mrs. Avery has a severe limp that makes her movements "cumbersome at best."

Suspect 3 (The Maid - Annie): Ruled out because she lacks the physical strength. Mrs. Avery noted Annie is too "feeble" to even help with heavy washing or lugging sheets, making it impossible for her to hack through thick wooden poles with a blade.

Suspect 4 (The Butler - Philips):

Motive: He was searching for the "treasure" rumored to be hidden on the grounds. He realized the treasure (the necklace) might have been sewn into the old clothes Mrs. Avery pulled from the attic to dress the scarecrows.

Behavioral Change: His "sudden burst of appetite" and loitering in the kitchen began four days ago—the exact same day the first scarecrow incident occurred and the day Mrs. Avery began using the old attic clothes.

The Opportunity: He was seen crossing the field from the house toward the scarecrow. He was "skinny but wiry," matching the "agile" description.

Physical Evidence: Inspector Gregson found a glittering necklace hidden among the spare attic clothes in the kitchen, proving that the old garments did indeed contain valuables.

The Logic: Philips slashed the scarecrows to search the clothing for the hidden necklace. By destroying them, he forced Mrs. Avery to make new ones using more clothes from the attic, allowing him to systematically "search" all the old garments.

The Reveal: The Inspector showed Philips the recovered necklace. Philips confessed, explaining he felt entitled to the "old master's" hidden value because he was locked out of the kitchen and couldn't search the clothes quietly.

Outcome: The Inspector suggested Philips return the necklace to Mr. Forbes and perhaps write a book about the "Scarecrow Slasher" to make the extra money he desired.
