The Incident: A foot-long, perfectly straight horizontal scratch was discovered on the driver's side door of Colonel Greenerbaum’s new Porsche Cayman S.

The Setting: The Porsche was parked facing down a steeply sloped driveway. Dr. Von Beddinglam’s car was parked next to it, facing up the slope toward the house.

Suspect 1 (Fido the Dog):

Accusation: The Colonel claimed Fido’s collar was at the same height as the scratch and that he hit the car while chasing a cat.

Elimination: The motion-sensor light only triggers when something is near the car/garage. When Fido first chased the cat, the light stayed off. Later, when Fido was near the house and saw the cat again, the light turned on. This proves Fido was never near the Porsche during the chase.

Suspect 2 (Roxie and her Bicycle):

Accusation: The Colonel claimed Roxie coasted between the cars and scratched the Porsche with her pedal.

Elimination: The driveway is "steeply sloped." To go up the driveway to the house (as Roxie did), one must pedal hard, not coast. Pedaling moves the pedal up and down, which would create a wavy or jagged mark, not a "straight as a ruler" horizontal scratch.

Suspect 3 (Malcolm and his Guitar):

Accusation: The Colonel demonstrated Malcolm scratching the car while walking from his mother’s car to the house, holding the guitar neck in his left hand.

Elimination: Malcolm is left-handed. He naturally carries his guitar in his right hand (to keep his dominant hand free or protected). As he walked from his mother’s car (facing up) to the house, the Porsche would have been on his left side. Since the guitar was in his right hand (the side away from the Porsche), it couldn't have made the scratch.

Suspect 4 (Colonel Greenerbaum):

The "Mud" Evidence: The Colonel licked his thumb and rubbed mud away from the scratch to show it was longer. If the scratch had happened in the driveway after the car was already muddy, the scratch would be on top of the mud (visible and clear). The fact that mud was covering the scratch proves the scratch existed before the car got muddy.

Motive/Knowledge: The Colonel knew the exact repair cost ($759.89) immediately, suggesting he had already received an estimate before arriving.

The Reveal: Rochester realized the Colonel was attempting to commit insurance fraud or bully his father into paying for pre-existing damage.
