“The car was already scratched when Colonel Greenerbaum got here. First, it could not have been Roxie with her bicycle. Because of how steep the driveway is, you have to pedal to get up it. That means it would not have created a level scratch. It could not have been Malcolm. Malcolm was left handed and carried the guitar in his right hand, but when he walked from his mother's car to the house the Porsche would have been on his left side. The Colonel was wrong in his demonstration. Since Fido did not trigger the motion sensor light when he chased the cat, he must not have been near the car. Since he was not near the car, he could not have scratched the car. That is why the other three could not have done it.”
“The Colonel knew it was scratched because he knew the exact cost to repair it. Not only that, but there was mud on the scratch. If the scratch had been made after the mud, the mud would not have been covering the scratch.”
