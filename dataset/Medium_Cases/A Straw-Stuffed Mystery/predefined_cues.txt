Crime Scene: Frank Albertson discovered his scarecrow ravaged and produce ruined. The "stabbing" marks and clean slashes indicated a wide-bladed knife was used rather than an animal attack.

Physical Evidence 1 (Sunflower Seed): The detective found a sunflower seed inside the scarecrow’s torso. Frank confirmed he does not grow sunflowers on his farm.

Physical Evidence 2 (Footprints): A footprint found at the scene matched the detective’s size 11 boot.

The Missing Weapon: Frank’s son, Bill, admitted to buying a hunting knife in town but claimed to have lost it. The last place he visited before heading home was Mr. Fletcher’s grocery store.

Suspect 1 (Professor Surenie): Ruled out because he was out of town attending a teacher's conference in Denver.

Suspect 2 (Rachel): Ruled out because her shoe size was significantly smaller than the size 11 footprint found at the scene.

Suspect 3 (Bill): Although he had a motive (a fight with his father) and owned the knife, his claim of losing the knife was corroborated by his father’s mention of Bill being upset about losing a new purchase.

Suspect 4 (Mr. Fletcher):

Motive: He harbored deep resentment toward Frank because a shipment of rotten vegetables had recently caused him significant financial loss.

Tell-tale Habit: During the investigation, Mr. Fletcher was seen repeatedly spitting sunflower seeds on the ground.

Opportunity: He found the hunting knife that Bill had accidentally left behind at the grocery store and used it to sabotage Frank's farm.

The Logical Flaw: Mr. Fletcher claimed to have been doing inventory at his store all night, but the presence of the sunflower seed (his habit) and his size 11 shoe print at the crime scene proved his presence at the farm.

The Reveal: The detective linked the discarded sunflower seed, the footprint size, and the access to the lost knife to identify Mr. Fletcher as the culprit.

Outcome: Mr. Fletcher confessed to the crime in a fit of rage and was arrested.
