The Crime: Jane Landau's iPod was stolen from her desk in Room 106 near the end of the second block.

Unique Identifiers: The stolen iPod had Jane’s name on the back and was adorned with various stickers, making it impossible to mistake for someone else's.

Suspect 1 (Shay Dulaney): She was sitting directly behind Jane. However, she was seen using her own iPod and reading at the end of class. The teacher deduced that if Shay were the thief, she wouldn't have used a stickered iPod in class without removing the stickers first to hide its identity.

Suspect 2 (Shawn Ramos): He has a history of theft, but he has a strong deterrent: he is one slip-up away from being expelled. He also provided a witness account that Lea was called to the office before the class ended.

Suspect 3 (Lea Thompson): She was sitting in front of Shawn. Both Shawn and the office records confirmed she was called down to the principal's office several minutes before the bell rang, removing her from the scene before the theft could have occurred.

Suspect 4 (Rachel Vermeer): She was sitting in front of Lea and was seen complaining about her father being "cheap" and not buying her things.

The Verbal Slip (The "Slip-up"): When questioned, Mrs. Adam mentioned that an iPod was stolen, without specifying whose it was. Rachel immediately claimed she "couldn't have taken Jane's iPod," revealing she knew exactly whose property was missing despite the teacher not naming the victim.

Motive: Rachel was angry with her father for not buying her an iPod, despite her good grades and chores, and felt she deserved one because "everyone else has one."

Physical Evidence: Upon searching Rachel's purse, Mrs. Adam found the iPod still adorned with stickers and the name "Jane Landau" on the back.

The Reveal: Rachel's specific knowledge of the victim's name before it was mentioned and the recovery of the stickered device from her bag confirmed her guilt.

Outcome: Rachel was sent to the principal's office, and the iPod was returned to Jane.
